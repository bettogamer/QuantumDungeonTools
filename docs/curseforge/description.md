# Quantum Dungeon Tools

**Know where your dungeon run stands: time against your record, bosses left, and what the run gave you.**

Quantum Dungeon Tools is a dungeon toolkit for WoW Forever, made by the Quantum guild. Its first
module, the **Instance Tracker**, sits at the side of your screen while you run a dungeon. It shows
how you are doing against your best time, which bosses are still alive, and the XP, gold and
reputation the run is giving you.

Built for WoW Forever (client 1.60). All the libraries it needs come inside: nothing else to install.

---

## A timer that knows your record

- **Run timer against your personal record**, per dungeon and difficulty, with a split and a
  +/- delta for every boss.
- **A time bar** marked with the splits of your record, so you see at a glance if you are ahead.
- **First time in a dungeon?** The bar turns into one segment per boss, and that run becomes your record.

## Everything about the run, in one place

- **Bosses and rares left**, with the time each one died. Rares are listed but never block the end of the run.
- **Deaths, XP gained, gold and reputation** of the run.
- **Mouseover detail**: XP and gold per hour, runs left to level up, reputation by faction, your
  records (also with your current group) and the instances you entered in the last hour.
- **Click a live boss** in the list to ping it for your group; right-click to mark it.

## Runs that follow the way you play

- **Leave and come back**: the clock pauses outside the dungeon and resumes when you return.
- **New copy or a reset** starts a new run on its own.
- **10 instances per hour**: a warning when you hit the limit, with the time until the next slot.
- **End-of-run card** with your time, record, deaths, XP, gold and reputation, and a button to
  share it with the group.

## Your way

- **Main window** (`/qdt` or the minimap icon) with the settings, a **live preview** of the
  widget and the **History** of your last 100 runs, filtered by dungeon, difficulty and character.
- **Turn off** any line of the widget, or use the **compact mode**: one line, with the boss list on mouseover.
- English and Spanish.

## Compatibility

WoW Forever only (`## Interface: 16001`). It does not support retail or Classic. Inside dungeons
the client gives addons no player position, so there is no map arrow.

## Feedback and bugs

This is a **beta**. Please report bugs and ideas in the **Comments** tab of this page: say what
you were doing and, if you can, paste the Lua error (BugSack helps).
